How Lab Color Space AI Works

Understanding the deep learning pipeline behind Zhang et al. ECCV 2016

1. L-Channel Extraction

The input B&W image represents the L (Lightness) channel. It is separated and scaled to 224×224 resolution for the neural network input.

2. Caffe DNN Forward Pass

An 8-block convolutional architecture predicts a probability distribution over 313 quantized color bins in the ab chrominance space.

3. Chrominance Fusion & Lab → BGR

The predicted a (Green-Red) and b (Blue-Yellow) channels are upscaled to full resolution, merged with original L, and converted back to RGB.
